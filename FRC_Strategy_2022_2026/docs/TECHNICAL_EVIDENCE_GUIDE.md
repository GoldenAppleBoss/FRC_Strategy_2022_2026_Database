# Reading technical evidence as an architecture agent

The mechanism notes separate five layers: published construction, the team's stated reason, original engineering interpretation, a proposed validation test, and remaining limits. The analysis is not an undisclosed quote from the team. A detailed explanation of why a contact or load path can work is not proof that the team measured that effect. When the source merely lists specifications, the stated-reason field explicitly says so. Do not convert a plausible mechanical explanation into a historical claim about the team's intent.

## A useful technical description follows the piece and the load

For an intake, identify the first powered contact, the supporting surface, the point where ownership becomes secure and every transition before storage. State the acquisition envelope and the failure when the piece arrives diagonally, at a wall, in a pile or at maximum approach speed. A wide mouth can have a narrow effective capture area if its outer contacts eject rather than center. A motor count does not reveal whether a dead zone interrupts ownership. Record material, wheel geometry, compression, power transmission, deployment kinematics and the structure that reacts an impact as separate facts.

For a transfer, follow the piece through narrowing, turning, orientation and staging. Each imposed constraint should remove an unwanted degree of freedom without adding an over-constrained state. An unpowered surface can be useful guidance or the dominant drag source. An agitator can release a bridge or repeatedly throw the piece away from the receiving contact. A funnel and a singulator solve different problems. Their useful rate must be evaluated at full, partial and nearly empty inventory, including an interrupted feed and a reverse.

For a shooter, identify the entry state, compression history, contact duration, wheel surface velocities, release angle and resulting translation and spin. Free wheel speed is not ball speed. A soft piece deforms and can slip; a flexible ring can enter the same nominal gap in several shapes. An additional backing roller can control spin while changing friction and packaging. The architecture should include a measured calibration process and the mechanical references that make it repeatable after service.

For a positioning mechanism, follow the force path from the piece through the end effector, joint, bearings, structure and chassis. Motor encoder accuracy does not measure output deflection or backlash. A stiff-looking plate can be attached through a flexible joint; a highly reduced gearbox can still have a weak final tooth or inadequate shaft support. Reducing mass far from an axis reduces inertia more than an equal reduction near it. Counterbalances reduce one load component while creating another angle-dependent force. State the complete motion and load condition before borrowing a ratio.

For a climber, distinguish acquisition, retention, lift, settling and power-off support. The load path changes during those phases. A hook that catches a rung is not necessarily the part that safely supports the final suspension. A winch that can lift the robot can still rotate it into an illegal or damaging contact if the chain line is far from the center of mass. Shared-joint climbs couple primary scoring damage to endgame reliability. In 2026 the strategic default remains fuel-first and low-priority climb, even when the hardware itself is interesting.

## First-order calculations are checks, not performance certifications

Surface speed is approximately `v = pi * D * RPM / 60` when diameter and time units are consistent. At the same shaft speed, changing diameter changes both surface speed and the torque needed to produce a given contact force. A reduction improves output torque while lowering output speed; a tooth-count pair must be interpreted in the team's stated driver/driven order. This corpus preserves source notation where direction is ambiguous. Do not silently invert it or turn a free-speed calculation into loaded performance.

For a rotating joint, gravity torque is approximately the sum of each mass times gravitational acceleration times its perpendicular moment arm. Dynamic torque also includes inertia times angular acceleration and friction. A long telescoping arm changes both moment arm and inertia through its stroke. A final reduction, shaft and bearing must react the load even when several motors share the input. Mechanical advantage can vary sharply through a linkage, so checking only the endpoints can miss the highest force.

Flywheel stored energy is `E = 0.5 * I * omega^2`. That explains why inertia can buffer a short launch impulse, but does not prove a heavier flywheel improves recovery or total score. Motor power must replace the average energy, and extra inertia costs spin-up energy and mass. Recovery also depends on the controller, transmission, compression and timing of the next ball. A shooter can perform well with low added inertia if its motor authority and contact state are appropriate. Preserve counterexamples rather than turning one champion's design into a universal prescription.

For a complete fuel path, the minimum capacity of its serial services provides only an optimistic ceiling. Arrival gaps, bursty supply, jams and motion can reduce output well below that minimum. A nearly empty hopper can expose a different bottleneck from a full one. Track delivered scored fuel per usable interval, not just bench BPS. For coral, track a full acquisition-to-next-acquisition cycle and the probability of a retained legal score. The fastest isolated joint motion is not automatically the fastest useful task.

## Tests should distinguish competing explanations

If an intake jams, record which contact first stops and whether a shaft, belt or guide moved. Change one compression or support dimension and reproduce the failed approach. If a shooter misses, separate localization error, hood-reference error, wheel recovery and ball-entry variation before changing the shot map. If a lift oscillates, measure both encoder tracking and physical tip motion. If an expanding hopper fails, test a loaded interrupted transition rather than only repeating empty endpoint moves.

Useful reported quantities include sample count, piece condition, battery voltage, configuration, setup and failure definition. Team-reported rates and movement times retain that label. A requirement table is not a results table; grey or unmet targets in a binder must not be imported as achievements. A CAD image is evidence of a design, not proof of a competed installation. A later rebuild must not overwrite the earlier robot. The all-125 binder audit distinguishes a located publication, accessible technical pages, a false association and an unresolved search; none establishes that every possible binder has been found.

## Recommendation output

Start with the game state and intended alliance role. State the measurable task goals, then name year/version-specific inspirations and the physical interfaces that must be redesigned. Include a positive reason and a failure mode for each major mechanism choice. Compare at least one coherent alternative under the same partner, supply, defense and development assumptions. Conclude with the smallest integrated experiment that could change the architecture decision. A recommendation that sounds fluent in team numbers but omits these conditions has not met the purpose of this database.

## 2. A supply field is harder to defend than one station

[Navigation 848–913 s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rFdIJfdh-Gs&t=848)

**Source account / attributed commentary:** The narrator’s assessment of floor intakes becomes more favorable as delivered coral spreads near the reef.

**Conditional interpretation — analyst reasoning:** Record this change in belief rather than rewriting the early forecast. A defender cannot cover every useful pickup with the same station-camping tactic. It needs resource displacement, route control or a different target.

## 3. Deny only resources that will become limiting

[Navigation 1414–1479 s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rFdIJfdh-Gs&t=1414)

**Source account / attributed commentary:** The account describes displaced coral that would matter to a faster alliance but not to one already supplied adequately for its output.

**Conditional interpretation — analyst reasoning:** Resource denial has zero or low marginal value while substitutes are abundant and accessible. Judge a defensive push by the next needed cycle and available alternatives, not the distance it moves a piece.

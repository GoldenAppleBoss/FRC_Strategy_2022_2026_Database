## 2. Autonomous marginal value depends on the expected final reef

[Navigation 1023–1088 s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hTpmhxZ5KyM&t=1023)

**Source account / attributed commentary:** The commentator considers central coral-plus-algae routines and the possibility of a nearly completed extra algae release. The prior full-caption account compares L3 and L4 autonomous premiums when branches would otherwise be filled in teleop.

**Conditional interpretation — analyst reasoning:** If both levels are ultimately completed, the relevant gain can be the autonomous premium plus displaced teleop time, not the entire printed coral value. That argument fails if the alliance will leave a higher branch empty. A near release is a development opportunity, not a scored piece.

## 3. A good defensive match can still lose on another phase

**Conditional interpretation — analyst reasoning:** Evaluate defense alongside autonomous reliability and endgame conversion. The outcome does not prove the defender was ineffective, and the tiebreak does not prove auto alone caused the loss. Preserve the competing explanations.

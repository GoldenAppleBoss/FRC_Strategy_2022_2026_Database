## Turning observations into goals

For an alliance-captain ambition, compare your expected pre-event capability to the event's strongest robots, then ask whether compatible partners can reach the observed alliance band. For a first/second-pick role, use the actual champion team EPA breakdown and identify the capability the alliance lacked. A robot well below the leading EPA can still be a winning defender, supplier or endgame partner. Conversely, matching the leader's EPA is insufficient if your autonomous conflicts or your architecture collapses under defense.

Set separate goals for the first event, the next improvement interval and DCMP. Use success distributions, not only a best run: acquisition/transfer time, accuracy under contact, successful endgame rate, downtime and repair duration. Recompute when RP thresholds or partner assumptions change. The CSV contains champion member EPAs and phase medians to make these comparisons explicit. In 2026, inspect tower fields separately from late fuel.

## 7769, 2026 CHUNK: flow, durability and honest failure history

Primary facts: the release describes a two-chamber shooter revised to a 2.75-width configuration, almost 70 nominal capacity and typically 50–60 in play. Team replies describe a static hood avoiding an exposed hood belt, and a feed jam corrected by stopping the lower indexing rollers. They also report rack-tooth breakage that persisted despite current-limit mitigation, with spares and repairs needed. Their linked technical slides currently display that the file was deleted. Those slides were not read. The retained mirror snapshot ranks 7769 fourth worldwide at EPA 309.29; it is a dated snapshot, not live verification. Official event results support an elite competitive season.

1833 transfer analysis: the useful principle is that usable match throughput is a system property. More capacity can add loaded inertia, acquisition dwell and feed pressure. More powered contact can make a congested path worse. Begin with a clear hypothesis about the limiting stage, instrument occupancy and delay at each transition, and change one stage before attributing a scoring gain. Test the near-empty, partly full and fully packed states separately. Under a defended shot, distinguish lost aim time, inaccessible geometry, supply starvation and a genuine mechanical jam. A simple hood can reduce interfaces and service work, but its advantages depend on the scoring aperture and collision environment of the new game.

The rack failure is an equally important lesson. Do not label every CHUNK component an optimized design merely because the robot performed exceptionally. Borrow the output discipline and diagnose its failure modes; do not recreate a known weak load path without a correction hypothesis and impact test. Preserve version labels so an early divided shooter and later wider arrangement are not combined into an imaginary single fielded version. The owner's desire to emulate 7769 means elite execution of a coherent primary scorer, not a requirement for a particular hopper size, rack intake or lane count in 2027.

## 1690, 2024 Doppler: compact primary scorer, substantial control development

Primary facts: the reveal gives an approximately 11-inch highest stowed point. Official results identify Orbit as a 2024 world champion. The mechanism is chassis-yaw aimed; its elevation/pivot motion is not a turret. In the CAD release, a team member warns that low packaging did not make every individual mechanism superior and that substantial work was needed. The team's controls explanation describes physically limited motion commands, skid/collision handling, driver assistance and automated shot readiness. Treat this as a team account rather than an independent performance decomposition.

1833 transfer analysis: low CG can increase usable acceleration and reduce settling, but free speed or stowed height alone cannot predict cycles. Compare time to accelerate, brake, reacquire heading, capture a piece and release a valid shot under contact. The cost of chassis aiming must be included in a non-turret architecture's state model. It may be small when acquisition exits with a useful heading and the driver can approach efficiently; it may be large when position and heading are constrained together. Geometry and software must be co-designed so that the driver requests remain physically feasible.

This example validates ambition with a compact architecture. It does not justify eliminating a necessary game function, assuming development is easy, or concluding that the lowest robot always wins. A low package that creates rare disabling piece poses may lose more match contribution than it gains in an ideal cycle. Preserve the existing note-deflector study as a reliability problem alongside the positive architecture example. The future design should carry an explicit optimization budget: time for loaded tuning, autonomous coordination, repeated defended acquisition, operator feedback and service drills. Hardware simplicity buys development capacity only if the saved effort is actually invested in those areas.

## 2056, 2025 LIGHTNING: familiar functions, engineered interfaces

Primary facts: OP describes choosing architecture early and selecting the simplest concept it thought could meet competitive goals, while noting that the robot was more demanding than desired and lacked a hanger at its first event. Its 33-page binder documents a ground intake, separate coral straightening/handoff stages, elevator, gripper and climb, with explicit sensing, service and stiffness details. Software and fabrication are documented as part of the robot, not omitted from the complexity account. The existing corpus already contains five technical notes from this binder. In this pass all 33 pages' extracted text was read; that does not certify every drawing visually. The source publication requests that its original documents not be rehosted; this edition links them and retains authored research notes rather than adding the source PDF.

1833 transfer analysis: a concept can be stated as an intake, elevator and wrist without being an easy robot to commission. The high-level simplicity resides in a short functional chain with clear ownership of each transformation, not in pretending that orientation, capture or controls are trivial. Decompose a coral cycle into acquisition, orientation, handoff, clearance, elevation, approach, release and retreat. Observe which stages can overlap without compromising retained state. An indexed piece that arrives consistently can save both actuator travel and repeated driver correction.

The relevant design comparison is not vacuum versus rollers in isolation. It is whether the complete capture-to-release system tolerates the expected piece poses and contacts while achieving repeatable short cycles. Evaluate a simpler effector's potential benefit against the additional upstream alignment it demands. A more constrained handoff may reduce end-effector burden but increase throat-jam sensitivity. Deliberately choose where the tolerance belongs and measure success across that interface. Similarly, service access and wiring restraint are output-preserving design functions; a nominally faster mechanism that repeatedly loses sensors or requires lengthy disassembly can have lower event contribution.

## 1690, 2025 WHISPER: compact functional description, coupled mechanics and execution

Primary facts: Orbit credits a closed-belt differential elevator reference and describes floor pickup as a cycle-time choice. Team posts identify pressure feedback and maintenance of an adhesive belt connection. Its championship recap discusses alliance interference and switching a partner to predictable loading-station lanes. A later intake-assist post explains driver-intent conditions and velocity correction. These accounts establish that optimization includes control and traffic management, not only a short mechanism list. They do not show that a differential or vacuum is universally simpler for another team.

1833 transfer analysis: separating moving hardware from actuator location can reduce inertia while creating motor-space coupling and calibration work. A differential should be assessed over simultaneous lift/pivot demands, saturation and failure recovery rather than judged by the number of motors saved. A vacuum can make a gripper visually small while moving complexity into sealing, pressure generation, state sensing and maintenance. Those are valid choices if they improve the whole cycle under the team's resources; they are not free complexity elimination.

For match modeling, faster individual floor pickup does not necessarily produce faster alliance throughput if robots fight over the same accessible pieces or routes. Compare shared supply and traffic policies explicitly. A robot designed to dominate can adopt a lane or temporary complementary assignment without having been designed as a weak support machine. The historical alliance discussion is therefore useful to 1833's elite ceiling even though support-only architecture is prohibited. Reserve time to test driver assistance at speed with realistic errors; a theoretically optimal command that surprises the driver may degrade acquisition. Observe driver workload and repeatability as well as mechanism motion time.

## 1261, 2025 Hatchet: preserve observation without laundering it into measured causality

Primary facts checked: official records identify the 2025 robot as Hatchet. The publisher's Behind the Bumpers description identifies a ground intake, fast arm, vision and automatic alignment. The interview is https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mAIFkK4kAE0; the transcript export reported no transcript and the player showed captions unavailable. The interview's full spoken mechanism account was therefore not reviewed. The team's public GitHub profile was located, but the inspected listing did not establish a 2025 robot repository. Do not confuse Suwanee Team 1261 with another Robo-Lions team.

Owner observation: the user regards 1261 as a less effective Orbit-like 2025 concept, without a climb and with indexing inconsistency. Preserve this as a knowledgeable PCH observer's comparison, not a newly verified all-season mechanism history or evidence that a particular part caused the difference. Public event results do not by themselves prove a missing mechanism or diagnose an indexing fault. Further primary CAD, team explanation, appropriately sampled match observations or failure logs would be needed to attribute the gap.

1833 transfer analysis: this is a valuable counterexample question: why can similar high-level functions yield substantially different results? Define comparable operating conditions and measure piece acquisition success by orientation; index completion and retry frequency; handoff dwell; scoring accuracy; traverse/settle time; autonomous success; maintenance downtime; and endgame contribution where valuable. Classify delays by the stage where they occur before changing architecture. A single jam clip is not a season-average failure rate, and a lower overall EPA cannot isolate indexing from alliance, role or endgame effects. The correct next step is a stage-by-stage comparison, not copying the stronger robot's silhouette or asserting inferiority as a fact. This study is deliberately supplemental and partial; it does not claim to add a complete 1261 dossier to the original selection cohort.

## Apply the lessons without assuming the 2027 game

Begin with the strongest primary-contributor objectives permitted by the new game. Compare a small number of coherent no-turret architectures. Prefer deleting a mechanism or powered interface when the same useful function can be achieved with fewer failure modes, but preserve a necessary capability even when it requires engineering. Invest saved complexity in sustained output, autonomous, acquisition quality, driver assistance, service readiness and iteration. Set measurable optimization experiments and retain the failures as prominently as the successes. Neither a simple concept nor a famous reference establishes performance until its interfaces and operating conditions have been evaluated.

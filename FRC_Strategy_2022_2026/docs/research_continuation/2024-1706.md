# 2024-1706: Two acquisition directions were the alternative to a turret

Focused study supplement. Not a full binder/CAD review, exhaustive subsystem specification, every-version history or visual interview watch.

## Published / retained construction account

The robot has front/rear acquisition, an extended leading roller for wall notes, and a pivoted differential-speed launcher. Reversing the staged note redirects it to the amp/trap carriage. Its swerve uses selectable gearing, with a middle setting in the filmed configuration.

## Team-stated reasoning or boundary

The team explicitly says turret complexity would burden the intake and handoff, so it chose two intakes instead. It reports fast local cleanup as a benefit; the brief amplified-period claim is a team account, not a universal four-note cycle benchmark.

## Engineering interpretation — analyst inference

Directional independence can be purchased at the collector instead of the launcher. This can reduce turning during pickup while retaining simpler aiming hardware, but adds a second collision face and more transfer states. The correct comparison is total useful scoring under the expected note distribution and defense, including reversal into the amp carriage.

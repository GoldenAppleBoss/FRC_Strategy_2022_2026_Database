# 2024-1796: A central selector and elevator trade travel for transfer complexity

Focused study supplement. Not a full binder/CAD review, exhaustive subsystem specification, every-version history or visual interview watch.

## Published / retained construction account

A nearly full-width intake centers notes with wedges. The central carriage can direct a note to the rear pivoting launcher or retain it for the three-stage internally belted amp/trap elevator. Separate launcher speeds create spin; printed supports maintain belt tension.

## Team-stated reasoning or boundary

The source connects the launcher speed difference to stable flight and describes selecting the destination in the middle of the robot. The retained source does not establish a numerical optimum for the offset frame geometry.

## Engineering interpretation — analyst inference

Central selection preserves task choice but creates a bidirectional handoff that must be indexed consistently. An elevator can place accurately at close targets while a rear launcher serves range; the benefit must exceed transfer delay, extra mass and the chance of a note straddling two mechanisms. Frame offsets also require checking the support polygon at elevated scoring poses.

## Proposed validation — not performed

Test direction reversals with a partially transferred note, measure elevator settle and belt tension drift, and verify the homing procedure after mechanical service.

## Limits

Focused technical passages and the retained dossier are the evidence scope. Exact event installation, complete CAD tolerances and all intermediate revisions are not certified.

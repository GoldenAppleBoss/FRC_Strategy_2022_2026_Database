# 2025-5990: Protected power and adjustable rigging support a ground-source cycle

Focused study supplement. Not a full binder/CAD review, exhaustive subsystem specification, every-version history or visual interview watch.

## Published / retained construction account

The two-stage floor intake keeps its motor inside the chassis, then uses a belt and centering wheel to orient coral. A fixture/beam-break locates the handoff. The continuous elevator has two motors and wrench-adjustable ratchet tensioners for its ropes; a rack positions the shared gripper.

## Team-stated reasoning or boundary

The team explicitly connects internal motor placement to preventing collision damage and the ratchet arrangement to maintaining rope tension. The interview also demonstrates vision-based coral acquisition.

## Engineering interpretation — analyst inference

A protected motor still transmits impact through shafts and belts, so the whole load path needs inspection. Adjustable rigging is useful when it makes a repeatable service procedure possible; excessive or unequal tension can add friction and rack a carriage. Vision-directed pickup should be evaluated with the mechanical centering tolerance rather than as an independent AI success.

## Proposed validation — not performed

Document a reproducible rope-tension procedure, check left/right travel under load, and test handoff after impacts. Measure acquisition success with occlusion and adjacent pieces.

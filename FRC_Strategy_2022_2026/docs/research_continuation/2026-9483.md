## Published / retained construction account

First-party replies describe separate X44s for indexer and spindexer, a spinning floor/cone with no deliberate wall compression, and multiple feeder-wheel and ratio trials. The third intake version adds a kicker and movable secondary kicker. The shooter lacks an upper roller; its moving-shot solution combines tuned lookup data and a physics calculation that compensates for spin.

## Team-stated reasoning or boundary

The team says the movable kicker resolves earlier collector difficulty and clarifies that the feeder delivers fuel without spin. It does not provide a definitive causal explanation for all of the achieved spindexer throughput. The added flood light helps close-range tag reliability rather than substantially increasing long-range detection.

## Engineering interpretation — analyst inference

The absence of a counter-roller makes spin an explicit aiming input rather than something to assume away. A compact turret may leave more free hopper area, but the detailed explanation of dead zones and geometry offered by another commenter is expressly an outsider’s hypothesis. Keep that hypothesis separate from the team’s replies. Successful self-unjamming also does not mean jams never occur; the source explicitly acknowledges occasional jams.

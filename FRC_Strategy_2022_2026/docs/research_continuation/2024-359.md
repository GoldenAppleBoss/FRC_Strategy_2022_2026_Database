# 2024-359: A shorter telescoping arm preserves direct note handling

Focused study supplement. Not a full binder/CAD review, exhaustive subsystem specification, every-version history or visual interview watch.

## Published / retained construction account

The arm retains an A-frame/shoulder architecture and reduces the telescope to one moving stage. A guided arm-end collector sends notes directly into the top/bottom-wheel launcher; extension is used for amp and trap. Multiple pose cameras and a note-detection camera support acquisition and scoring.

## Team-stated reasoning or boundary

The team explicitly says a third stage was unnecessary for the required reach. It identifies avoiding an amp handoff as a reason for consistent amp operation.

## Engineering interpretation — analyst inference

Reducing extension stages can remove mass, cable length, backlash and maintenance points while retaining the needed task envelope. The comparison must use actual amp/trap geometry and the stage clearance rules, not maximum theoretical arm reach. Direct note handling avoids transfer errors but carries the launcher’s mass and flexible note through the arm’s motion.

## Proposed validation — not performed

Verify reachable poses and structural deflection at the actual amp/trap approach. Test note retention during fast shoulder motion and clearance under the stage without relying on perfect driver positioning.

Focused technical passages and the retained dossier are the evidence scope. Exact event installation, complete CAD tolerances and all intermediate revisions are not certified.

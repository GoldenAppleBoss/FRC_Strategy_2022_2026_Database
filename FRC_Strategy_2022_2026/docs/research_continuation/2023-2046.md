# 2023-2046: Low arm mass and repeatable collect stops support a large reach envelope

Focused study supplement. Not a full binder/CAD review, exhaustive subsystem specification, every-version history or visual interview watch.

## Published / retained construction account

A lightweight shoulder/elbow arm with low transmissions carries a three-roller claw. The collect pose bears against a rigid bumper support and stays contained. The team reports an approximately 17-pound arm assembly; module selection also considered ground clearance and packaging.

## Team-stated reasoning or boundary

The source explicitly emphasizes reducing mass given the required outward/upward reach and credits Citrus-style claw ideas while making the implementation smaller and lighter. Cube compression is chosen to retain rather than damage the piece.

## Engineering interpretation — analyst inference

A mechanical collect stop can define acquisition geometry more repeatably than a high-gain servo alone, provided the bumper/support does not move unpredictably. Reducing distal mass improves torque demand and balance, but must preserve stiffness at maximum reach. Ground-clearance decisions also affect charge-station behavior and any intended special climbing approach.

## Proposed validation — not performed

Measure deflection at full reach, test pickup after bumper displacement, and verify retention across both piece types and abrupt chassis motion.

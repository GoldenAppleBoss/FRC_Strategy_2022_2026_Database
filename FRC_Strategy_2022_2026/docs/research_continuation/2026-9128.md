# 2026-9128: Trench access and speed-error feeding explain the rebuild

Focused study supplement. Not a full binder/CAD review, exhaustive subsystem specification, every-version history or visual interview watch.

## Published / retained construction account

A slotted three-roller transfer provides adjustable compression before a two-X60 high-inertia launcher. The flexible Hopper Flopper compresses under the trench and re-expands afterward. The shooter was reversed relative to the original robot, and the feeder speed varies with launcher speed error. The interview explicitly reports no climb.

## Team-stated reasoning or boundary

The team links trench access to losing neutral-zone fuel races, preserving capacity to avoiding a smaller fixed hopper, and reversed orientation to reducing the need to turn after acquisition. Transfer modulation is intended to give fuel sufficient energy transfer while maintaining accuracy.

## Engineering interpretation — analyst inference

A compliant storage envelope changes which routes are available, so its value cannot be measured only in added fuel. It can change autonomous arrival order and the opponent’s available supply. Speed-error-dependent feed is a form of demand management: slow entry when the launcher is behind instead of allowing a nominally high feed command to create low-energy misses. The caption’s flywheel mass arithmetic is inconsistent, so preserve the qualitative high-inertia claim and seek a published mass breakdown rather than silently choosing one number.

## Proposed validation — not performed

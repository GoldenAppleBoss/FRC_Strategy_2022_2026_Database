# 2024-6328: Routing the note and routing the chain are both architecture interfaces

Focused study supplement. Not a full binder/CAD review, exhaustive subsystem specification, every-version history or visual interview watch.

## Published / retained construction account

A full-width collector uses leading rollers, wedges and slippery passive centering surfaces. The indexer selects the main shooter or rack-driven amp/trap backpack. Double-sided belts package opposite roller directions. Climb-side fangs guide the chain over areas where it could catch the shooter/backpack; four pose cameras use deliberately different views/lenses.

## Team-stated reasoning or boundary

The source explicitly connects the fangs to avoiding chain entrapment and the camera arrangement to useful tag visibility on approach to scoring locations. It treats camera placement as a deliberate strategy choice rather than simply adding identical cameras.

## Engineering interpretation — analyst inference

A compact scorer and climber can each work separately but fail when a flexible chain sweeps through their shared space. Passive routing surfaces are therefore a mechanism interface, not cosmetic guards. Similarly, extra cameras help only when their sight lines and uncertainty provide information the existing views lack.

## Proposed validation — not performed

Test chain capture with lateral error and robot roll, inspect every backpack handoff, and measure pose availability along the intended routes rather than only at a parked test position.

Focused technical passages and the retained dossier are the evidence scope. Exact event installation, complete CAD tolerances and all intermediate revisions are not certified.

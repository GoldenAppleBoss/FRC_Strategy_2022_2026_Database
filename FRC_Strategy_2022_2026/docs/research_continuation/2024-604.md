# 2024-604: Controlled centering preserves deliberately asymmetric shooting contact

Focused study supplement. Not a full binder/CAD review, exhaustive subsystem specification, every-version history or visual interview watch.

## Published / retained construction account

A wide compliant/dead-axis intake and guides center the note before an asymmetric launcher contact sequence. The same scoring assembly handles amp delivery and rises for trap work. Beam-break acquisition feedback retracts the collector and rumbles the driver controller.

## Team-stated reasoning or boundary

The team identifies driver tolerance, source pickup speed and long-range scoring that reduces driving as priorities. The source reports about a second of cycle improvement from acquisition feedback/retraction behavior; that is a team estimate, not an isolated controlled trial.

## Engineering interpretation — analyst inference

Ring deformation and lateral offset can change which launcher contact happens first. Centering is therefore part of generating the intended spin, not merely getting the note somewhere inside the robot. The driver-feedback example also shows how a small state signal can reduce wasted acquisition motion without changing motor power.

## Proposed validation — not performed

Vary lateral entry, note wear and intake speed; measure spin, trajectory spread and complete cycle time. Separate the effect of a wider pickup from the effect of better feedback.

## Limits

Focused technical passages and the retained dossier are the evidence scope. Exact event installation, complete CAD tolerances and all intermediate revisions are not certified.

# 2024-1756: Omni centering and reoriented transfer need defined staging states

Focused study supplement. Not a full binder/CAD review, exhaustive subsystem specification, every-version history or visual interview watch.

## Published / retained construction account

Omni wheels and printed wedges center the note while allowing lateral motion. The rotating shooter aligns with the intake to receive it; paired sensors detect travel and possession. The same moving assembly uses gentle roller release for amp/trap rather than a high-speed shot.

## Team-stated reasoning or boundary

The team identifies lateral freedom as the reason for the omni contact choice. Sensor feedback reduces the driver’s need to see the note and helps avoid acquiring a second piece.

## Engineering interpretation — analyst inference

A roller can create useful forward traction while resisting necessary lateral centering. Omni contact addresses that conflict, but the wedges still establish the note’s final alignment. A moving transfer interface adds a temporal constraint: feeding must wait for receiving geometry, then stop at a verified state before reorientation. Gentler release for a confined goal is a separate control mode from speaker firing.

## Proposed validation — not performed

Test diagonal entries and sensor disagreement; measure capture/transfer tolerances at the rotating interface and retention during the following motion.

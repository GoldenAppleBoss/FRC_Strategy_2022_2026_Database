## Published / retained construction account

A wide four-bar collector feeds the lightweight coral/algae effector on a string-driven continuous elevator. The interview describes thicker carbon after a week-five collector member bent and cracked; the caption does not safely establish the thickness. Later cage guides and capture wheels replace a slower alignment approach.

## Team-stated reasoning or boundary

The team links width to driver tolerance, elevator construction to speed/weight/CG, and the revised cage capture to reducing an approximately twenty-second climb process toward roughly ten seconds.

## Engineering interpretation — analyst inference

The motorized scoring cycle is only one part of match throughput. Wide acquisition can reduce driver correction, while a faster endgame can recover entire scoring cycles. Those gains can justify mass in guides or reinforcement even when they increase subsystem weight. The original and revised climb should not be averaged into one time, and the gain should not be applied to a team whose approach geometry differs.

## Proposed validation — not performed

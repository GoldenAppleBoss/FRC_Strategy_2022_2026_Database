## Published / retained construction account

The fourth arm/grabber iteration works beside a sideways-oriented elevator. The robot handles coral levels and algae with the same arm assembly. The interview also describes placing the battery to counter the elevator’s mass and moving the scoring arm clear during climbing.

## Team-stated reasoning or boundary

The team explicitly says it wanted pass-through behavior but did not consider passing through the elevator suitable for its resources. It identifies low center of gravity as a reason for the alternate arrangement.

## Engineering interpretation — analyst inference

Pass-through is a functional requirement, not a mandate to send the piece through an elevator opening. An offset mechanism can provide the desired acquisition/scoring directions while changing lateral load, frame torsion and the safe sweep envelope. Counterweight placement may improve static balance but should be checked throughout arm travel and with each game piece.

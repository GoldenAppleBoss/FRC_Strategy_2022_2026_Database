# 2023-1756: Deployable bash guards protect a collector but can obstruct scoring

Focused study supplement. Not a full binder/CAD review, exhaustive subsystem specification, every-version history or visual interview watch.

## Published / retained construction account

An Everybot-style roller gripper and flipping wrist acquire cones from either direction. Deployable side bash guards move with the exposed collector. Lift and wrist motion overlap to reduce delay, while driver modes control guard behavior.

## Team-stated reasoning or boundary

The team explicitly says preventing damage is preferable to repeatedly replacing the collector. It also notes that guards should not deploy during scoring because they would contact the wall.

## Engineering interpretation — analyst inference

A protective structure changes the collision envelope it is intended to manage. It can improve floor acquisition under defense while creating a new field-contact risk near the grid. The safe state therefore includes the guards, not only the arm and gripper. Overlapped motions save time only if their intermediate envelopes remain clear.

## Proposed validation — not performed

Test lateral collector impacts, scoring-wall clearance and interrupted transitions. Measure full pickup-to-release time with the guard interlocks active.

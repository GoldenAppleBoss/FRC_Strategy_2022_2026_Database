# 2023-111: Symmetric acquisition and sufficient release clearance are different requirements

Focused study supplement. Not a full binder/CAD review, exhaustive subsystem specification, every-version history or visual interview watch.

## Published / retained construction account

A vertically symmetric gripper acquires cubes and upright/tipped cones from either robot face. A continuous lift uses synchronized sides, guides and adjustable structural stays. The interview reports redesigning the claw to open farther because cubes remained trapped after the release command.

## Team-stated reasoning or boundary

The team explicitly ties gripper symmetry to the arm working on both sides. The larger opening responds to failed cube release rather than poor initial acquisition.

## Engineering interpretation — analyst inference

A mechanism that holds both pieces well can still release one poorly. Compliant foam and tapered cones create different contact/retention conditions, so acquisition success is not a substitute for a release test. Through-robot arm travel also makes lift parallelism and cable clearance part of both scoring directions.

## Proposed validation — not performed

Measure release delay and remaining contact for worn cubes, test cone orientation extremes, and check lift binding after frame contact.

## Limits

Focused technical passages and the retained dossier are the evidence scope. Exact event installation, complete CAD tolerances and all intermediate revisions are not certified.

# 2023-2075: Backlash and compliant handoff tolerance must be separated

Focused study supplement. Not a full binder/CAD review, exhaustive subsystem specification, every-version history or visual interview watch.

## Published / retained construction account

A floor beater/roller collector hands pieces to a shoulder/elbow arm and claw. The source describes high shoulder loads and early slop problems. The wider claw accommodates variable lateral arrival and can also acquire at the shelf.

## Team-stated reasoning or boundary

The team ties a rigid shoulder transmission to consistent placement and the wide/compliant receiver to successful handoff from a broad collector.

## Engineering interpretation — analyst inference

Useful compliance at the receiver is different from uncontrolled angular lost motion at the shoulder. One enlarges acquisition tolerance; the other shifts the end-effector position unpredictably at the node. A good transfer should center or otherwise define the retained piece before the arm uses precision scoring poses.

## Proposed validation — not performed

Measure piece-position spread after handoff separately from arm-position hysteresis. Test both shelf and ground acquisition, including incomplete transfers and reversals.

## Limits

Focused technical passages and the retained dossier are the evidence scope. Exact event installation, complete CAD tolerances and all intermediate revisions are not certified.

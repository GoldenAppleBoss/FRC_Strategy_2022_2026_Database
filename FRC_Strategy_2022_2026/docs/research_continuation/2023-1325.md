# 2023-1325: Remote wrist actuation and practiced direct pickup avoid a second collector

Focused study supplement. Not a full binder/CAD review, exhaustive subsystem specification, every-version history or visual interview watch.

## Published / retained construction account

A small square chassis carries a shoulder/elbow/wrist arm with low-mounted transmissions and a remote aircraft-cable wrist drive. Fixed gripper spacing handles both pieces. A separate Rapid React-style collector was prototyped but not adopted; arm motion uses waypoint/path-following ideas related to the drivetrain tools.

## Team-stated reasoning or boundary

The team connects chassis size to maneuverability and says the added collector was unnecessary after substantial practice with the existing gripper, with integration and retraining costs outweighing expected benefit.

## Engineering interpretation — analyst inference

A theoretical faster acquisition mechanism can be a worse late-season choice if it consumes development and driver time without improving full-cycle reliability. Remote cable drive reduces distal motor mass but makes tension, stretch and backlash important. Safe endpoint waypoints also do not certify the swept volume between them.

## Proposed validation — not performed

Measure wrist hysteresis, arm clearance throughout each path, and direct-pickup performance under realistic misalignment before reviving the rejected collector.

## Limits

Focused technical passages and the retained dossier are the evidence scope. Exact event installation, complete CAD tolerances and all intermediate revisions are not certified.

# 2024-1771: Low moving mass and rapid prototyping support a multi-task pivot

Focused study supplement. Not a full binder/CAD review, exhaustive subsystem specification, every-version history or visual interview watch.

## Published / retained construction account

A protected under-bumper collector and polycord conveyor feed a central pivoted scoring assembly. Motors sit near the pivot and long belt runs carry power outward. Independent shooting sides provide note spin; the same mechanism serves ground pickup, speaker and amp poses.

## Team-stated reasoning or boundary

The source describes selecting objectives at kickoff, prototyping multiple launcher types and using short print/test iterations to resolve problems. Its under-bumper choice emphasizes collision protection.

## Engineering interpretation — analyst inference

Moving motors toward the pivot can reduce rotational inertia, but long belts introduce compliance, tension and replacement considerations. A shared pivot simplifies the number of mechanisms while making each pose and connecting motion more important. Rapid printed iterations are a development capability that another team must budget for, rather than assume it receives by copying the final CAD.

## Proposed validation — not performed

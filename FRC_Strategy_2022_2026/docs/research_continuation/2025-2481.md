# 2025-2481: Floor orientation and a moving scorer share the alignment workload

Focused study supplement. Not a full binder/CAD review, exhaustive subsystem specification, every-version history or visual interview watch.

## Published / retained construction account

The collector uses one motor, a passive guide wall and wheel/roller contact to orient floor coral without a separate vertical-roller stage. A chain-driven lateral slide on Igus guides corrects scorer position. A cascade elevator and custom drivetrain complete the retained architecture.

## Team-stated reasoning or boundary

The team explicitly connects floor pickup to human players rolling coral near the reef. Its custom-module discussion emphasizes traction and useful gearing rather than adopting an unmodified commercial package.

## Engineering interpretation — analyst inference

A passive orienter exchanges actuator count for sensitivity to incoming pose, wall friction and driving direction. A lateral scorer can remove some last-meter chassis correction, but it must be stiff enough that the camera’s intended offset corresponds to the actual end-effector position. Ground-source supply and local correction should therefore be assessed as one cycle, including the human player’s delivery error.

## Proposed validation — not performed

Sample many coral approach orientations and supply distances. Measure lateral-slide backlash/deflection under acceleration, and compare whole-cycle time against a fixed scorer with chassis-only alignment.

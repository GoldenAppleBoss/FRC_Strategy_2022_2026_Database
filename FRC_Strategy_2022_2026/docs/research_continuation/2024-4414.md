## Team-stated reasoning or boundary

The team reports structural problems affecting under-bumper acquisition and insufficient original turret power for its preferred long shot. It decided the turret’s advantages did not outweigh its costs for the intended robot functions.

## Engineering interpretation — analyst inference

This is a strategic architecture rejection with concrete technical causes. A turret is not automatically superior when its packaging reduces stiffness, collection quality or release capability. The fixed-heading replacement must still pay chassis-aiming time and defended heading constraints, so the reason applies to the team’s specific route/shot strategy rather than every Crescendo robot.

# 2024-1690: A small robot still needs protection against rare disabling note poses

Focused study supplement. Not a full binder/CAD review, exhaustive subsystem specification, every-version history or visual interview watch.

## Published / retained construction account

A compact custom-swerve robot feeds its launcher through pivot-concentric transmission elements. The retained launcher uses four horizontal contacts and a rack-based angle mechanism. A note deflector was added before championships after notes lodged beneath the shooter in two losses; the climb controller corrects roll and coordinates trap release with swing.

## Team-stated reasoning or boundary

The team explicitly contrasts the season’s small/simple objective with the prior year’s complexity. The deflector is a response to two specific disabling events, not a decorative cover.

## Engineering interpretation — analyst inference

Rare jams can dominate an otherwise excellent scoring architecture because they remove the whole robot’s contribution. Preventing entry to a bad configuration can be more reliable than detecting and reversing it later. Concentric feeding preserves operation across launcher angles, but actual clearances and moving power transmission still need review. Trap timing based on swing is a separate dynamic-control problem, not proof of static shot accuracy.

## Proposed validation — not performed

Reproduce the reported under-shooter trap pose, test the deflector with damaged notes, and validate swing/roll compensation under asymmetric chain capture.

## Limits

Focused technical passages and the retained dossier are the evidence scope. Exact event installation, complete CAD tolerances and all intermediate revisions are not certified.

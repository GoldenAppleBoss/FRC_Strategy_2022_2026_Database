# 2024-2075: Compliant bumper entry and a supported pivot solve separate uncertainties

Focused study supplement. Not a full binder/CAD review, exhaustive subsystem specification, every-version history or visual interview watch.

## Published / retained construction account

Spring-loaded under-bumper collector segments compress notes through a limited opening, with an additional over-bumper collector. A sensor-tracked conveyor stages the note. The launcher angle is driven on both sides, and the source describes protecting the separate position sensor/transmission from damage.

## Team-stated reasoning or boundary

The team connects the two-sided pivot drive to reducing wobble and describes launcher changes made to gain useful shooting distance. The independent collector segments are intended to acquire at a wall as well as in open space.

## Engineering interpretation — analyst inference

Compliant entry can increase tolerance while making the note’s shape and arrival speed variable. The downstream stage must restore a repeatable state before release. A symmetric pivot support helps angular repeatability, but the stiffness of the frame and belt/chain tension still determine the actual output angle.

## Proposed validation — not performed

Measure entry success at bumper contact, note deformation after transport, and left/right pivot deflection under acceleration. Verify stage sensing before comparing range.

## Limits

Focused technical passages and the retained dossier are the evidence scope. Exact event installation, complete CAD tolerances and all intermediate revisions are not certified.

# 2023-1577: A compliant collector must hand off at the actual revised arm geometry

Focused study supplement. Not a full binder/CAD review, exhaustive subsystem specification, every-version history or visual interview watch.

## Published / retained construction account

A low-pivot collector uses mecanum/omni contact to center a tipped cone, then rotates it toward a narrow gripper. The extending scoring arm keeps its transmission low and measures actual arm angle beyond gearbox backlash. Shoulder-style bearing support helps retain bearings in flexible plates.

## Team-stated reasoning or boundary

The team explicitly credits Grasshoppers inspiration, seeks fast/light centering and explains the low collector pivot as letting impacts lift the mechanism rather than break it. Handoff angle changes must follow hardware revisions.

## Engineering interpretation — analyst inference

A wide pickup can improve acquisition while increasing the range of states presented to a narrow receiver. Compliance helps survive contact but must not erase the repeatable pose needed for transfer. Measuring the joint after transmission backlash improves observability; it does not mechanically remove the backlash.

## Proposed validation — not performed

Test handoff after collector impacts, compare actual and motor-side angle under reversals, and verify bearing retention after repeated plate flex.

Focused study supplement. Not a full binder/CAD review, exhaustive subsystem specification, every-version history or visual interview watch.

## Published / retained construction account

A full-width under-bumper collector uses independently driven lateral funneling into a protected ramp/tunnel. The retained source record describes a low rear launcher pivot with separate left/right shooting groups and the same assembly serving amp delivery.

## Team-stated reasoning or boundary

The interview emphasizes preserving a continuous note journey and designing for high-level strategic options early. It does not establish that every apparent feature was added specifically for one later playoff tactic.

## Engineering interpretation — analyst inference

A low tunnel can limit note bending and moving mass, but a protective cover also needs to avoid becoming a new trapping surface. Separately powered funneling allows transport behavior to be tuned without forcing the ground pickup to the same speed. The useful reference is a consistent note state at release; a compact exterior alone does not establish that consistency.

## Proposed validation — not performed

Test note distortion, off-center entry and cover clearances with worn notes. Compare shot variation after aggressive collection and after gentle hand placement to isolate transport effects.

# 2025-1771: Wrist freedom creates L1 value and a collision-planning obligation

Focused study supplement. Not a full binder/CAD review, exhaustive subsystem specification, every-version history or visual interview watch.

## Published / retained construction account

The team explicitly credits 4414’s 2023 continuous elevator. Its carriage combines a pivot, wrist and compliant coral/algae end effector; range sensors and a transverse wheel actively center coral. The software models tested safe poses and allows only transitions connected in a graph.

## Team-stated reasoning or boundary

The interview ties the wrist to effective L1 cycles and the pivot to serving both ground and station acquisition. The restricted transition graph is intended to prevent destructive arm/elevator interactions and overshoot.

## Engineering interpretation — analyst inference

The wrist should not be justified only by branch reach. It also changes how coral can be presented to the trough, so its value can rise in a low-task alliance role. The price is a larger configuration space: correct endpoint poses do not prove that the path between them is safe. A tested edge graph is a concrete way to carry mechanical clearance assumptions into software.

## Proposed validation — not performed

Test every allowed transition under lag, sensor error and simultaneous chassis motion. Measure L1 throughput as well as L4 reach, and verify active centering before the final placement motion.

## Limits

Focused technical passages and the retained dossier are the evidence scope. Exact event installation, complete CAD tolerances and all intermediate revisions are not certified.

## Team-stated reasoning or boundary

The team explicitly identifies difficult station alignment under defense and successful floor pickups on 1778/2046 as motivations. The offset is described as allowing algae removal and coral placement in one fluid transition.

## Engineering interpretation — analyst inference

A floor collector should be evaluated as a new cycle source, not an isolated pickup demo. The existing arm must keep coral controlled through the algae motion, and mass must be recovered elsewhere without creating excessive compliance. The six-inch separation is an interface dimension for that combined motion; it is not a universal clearance between any two manipulators.

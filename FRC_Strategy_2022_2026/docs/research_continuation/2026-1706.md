# 2026-1706: Twin spindexers trade packaging freedom for multiple coupled interfaces

Focused study supplement. Not a full binder/CAD review, exhaustive subsystem specification, every-version history or visual interview watch.

## Published / retained construction account

The mechanism has two rotating floor/cone systems and uptake ramps, separately controlled kickers, independent turrets and hood adjustment. The interview describes X44-driven indexer systems, 6-inch aluminum flywheels replacing heavy steel trials, an under-mounted battery on a pinned removable panel, and chassis-speed-dependent intake voltage bounded around 6.5–12 V.

## Team-stated reasoning or boundary

Aluminum flywheels were chosen after the steel trial proved too heavy. The battery mounting responds to crowded internal packaging. The retained interview account describes bristles, stacked wheels and shaped cones as successive feed iterations.

## Engineering interpretation — analyst inference

Two aiming channels do not automatically double match output. Fuel must be distributed to both cones, delivered through both ramps and accelerated without one path starving or overpacking. Moving-shot math must use the location of each turret rather than a single robot-center vector; angular chassis motion creates different local velocities at separated release points. Dynamic collector speed can match acquisition to chassis motion, but the exact speed signal and interpolation should be checked in code because the caption wording mixes angular and translational quantities. The removable battery panel is part of the architecture: dense packaging is only useful if the robot can still be serviced reliably.

## Proposed validation — not performed

Test unequal hopper loading, one jammed lane, low battery, turning shots and underbody battery retention after bump contact. Verify the actual intake-speed input in source code before reproducing the controller.

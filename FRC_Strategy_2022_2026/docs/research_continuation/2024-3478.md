# 2024-3478: Consistent staging is needed before a multi-pose launcher can be accurate

Focused study supplement. Not a full binder/CAD review, exhaustive subsystem specification, every-version history or visual interview watch.

## Published / retained construction account

A roller collector feeds a compliant-wheel launcher that rises on an elevator for amp and trap. Three distance sensors establish note position; magnetic switches protect climb travel. Trap execution changes hooks, elevator height and launcher angle in a defined sequence.

## Team-stated reasoning or boundary

The source explicitly connects the distance sensors to placing the note consistently and the limit devices to preventing mechanism damage. More exact intake material/ratio details are not established by the selected caption passages.

## Engineering interpretation — analyst inference

One launcher serving several targets reduces duplicate release hardware but changes gravity, retention and moving mass across poses. A correct angle table assumes a repeatable note position. The climb/trap sequence must also avoid moving the launcher through the chain or frame while partially loaded.

## Proposed validation — not performed

Test staged-note position spread, sensor disagreement and retention during elevator motion. Validate the complete trap sequence with realistic swing and lateral approach error.

# 2023-973: Commercial structure and deletion of an unnecessary roller reduce development work

Focused study supplement. Not a full binder/CAD review, exhaustive subsystem specification, every-version history or visual interview watch.

## Published / retained construction account

The robot adapts a WCP GreyT elevator with two Falcons and a narrower configuration. The gripper evolves from side-grabbing concepts toward Everybot-style upper cone/lower cube contacts; an extra powered roller is removed after tests showed it was unnecessary.

## Team-stated reasoning or boundary

The team explicitly identifies commercial elevator parts as compatible with its manufacturing resources and describes the third roller as large and awkward without sufficient benefit. Swerve adoption was preceded by offseason work.

## Engineering interpretation — analyst inference

The useful reference is a tested simplification, not a claim that commercial hardware requires no engineering. Narrowing a standard structure changes bearing loads, stiffness and packaging. Removing a contact is justified when the remaining contacts retain acquisition and release tolerance across real piece conditions.

## Proposed validation — not performed

Test the narrowed elevator for racking, validate pickup after collector deletion with worn pieces, and include offseason drive-training time in any comparison.

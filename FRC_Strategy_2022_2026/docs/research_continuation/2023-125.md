# 2023-125: Split rack collectors preserve a central arm corridor

Focused study supplement. Not a full binder/CAD review, exhaustive subsystem specification, every-version history or visual interview watch.

## Published / retained construction account

Two cantilevered rack-and-pinion collector halves leave space for a telescoping arm, synchronized by a cross-shaft with service couplers. The shoulder transmission is nested below the arm; a compact wrist and roller claw complete direct scoring.

## Team-stated reasoning or boundary

The team identifies robust, controllable deployment and avoiding pneumatics as reasons for the rack choice. Shoulder placement is tied to maximizing useful stowed arm length.

## Engineering interpretation — analyst inference

The center gap is not wasted intake width: it is clearance purchased for another mechanism. Synchronization must keep both collector halves from racking while still permitting fast replacement. As the arm extends, gravity torque rises with lever arm, so tuning only the retracted shoulder does not establish controllability at high reach.

## Proposed validation — not performed

Test one-sided collector impacts, service-coupler realignment and shoulder tracking at maximum extension with each game piece.

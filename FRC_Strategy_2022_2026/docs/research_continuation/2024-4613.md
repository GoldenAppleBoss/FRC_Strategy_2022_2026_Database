# 2024-4613: Different contact stiffness enables amp delivery without a pivot

Focused study supplement. Not a full binder/CAD review, exhaustive subsystem specification, every-version history or visual interview watch.

## Published / retained construction account

The under-bumper collector and guides feed two independently driven launcher rollers. The interview describes a lower-durometer lower contact relative to the upper contact, combined with speed control, to curve the amp delivery without moving the whole launcher. A deflector protects the climber from dropped notes.

## Team-stated reasoning or boundary

The team explicitly chose the contact/speed arrangement to perform both speaker and amp tasks without a pivot, with robustness as a stated benefit. The climber guard responds to the possibility of a falling note disabling it.

## Engineering interpretation — analyst inference

Eliminating an angle actuator shifts trajectory control into contact deformation, spin and launch speed. That reduces joints and moving wiring, but may increase sensitivity to note wear, temperature and compression. The guard illustrates that a rare dropped piece can create a structural failure state even when the main transport path works.

## Proposed validation — not performed

Map amp accuracy with used notes, varying voltage and repeated shooting. Test the dropped-note guard from several directions and verify that it does not obstruct normal climbing.

## 7769, 2026 CHUNK: flow, durability and honest failure history

Primary facts: the release describes a two-chamber shooter revised to a 2.75-width configuration, almost 70 nominal capacity and typically 50–60 in play. Team replies describe a static hood avoiding an exposed hood belt, and a feed jam corrected by stopping the lower indexing rollers. They also report rack-tooth breakage that persisted despite current-limit mitigation, with spares and repairs needed. Their linked technical slides currently display that the file was deleted. Those slides were not read. The retained mirror snapshot ranks 7769 fourth worldwide at EPA 309.29; it is a dated snapshot, not live verification. Official event results support an elite competitive season.

1833 transfer analysis: the useful principle is that usable match throughput is a system property. More capacity can add loaded inertia, acquisition dwell and feed pressure. More powered contact can make a congested path worse. Begin with a clear hypothesis about the limiting stage, instrument occupancy and delay at each transition, and change one stage before attributing a scoring gain. Test the near-empty, partly full and fully packed states separately. Under a defended shot, distinguish lost aim time, inaccessible geometry, supply starvation and a genuine mechanical jam. A simple hood can reduce interfaces and service work, but its advantages depend on the scoring aperture and collision environment of the new game.

The rack failure is an equally important lesson. Do not label every CHUNK component an optimized design merely because the robot performed exceptionally. Borrow the output discipline and diagnose its failure modes; do not recreate a known weak load path without a correction hypothesis and impact test. Preserve version labels so an early divided shooter and later wider arrangement are not combined into an imaginary single fielded version. The owner's desire to emulate 7769 means elite execution of a coherent primary scorer, not a requirement for a particular hopper size, rack intake or lane count in 2027.

## 1261, 2025 Hatchet: preserve observation without laundering it into measured causality

Primary facts checked: official records identify the 2025 robot as Hatchet. The publisher's Behind the Bumpers description identifies a ground intake, fast arm, vision and automatic alignment. The interview is https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mAIFkK4kAE0; the transcript export reported no transcript and the player showed captions unavailable. The interview's full spoken mechanism account was therefore not reviewed. The team's public GitHub profile was located, but the inspected listing did not establish a 2025 robot repository. Do not confuse Suwanee Team 1261 with another Robo-Lions team.

Owner observation: the user regards 1261 as a less effective Orbit-like 2025 concept, without a climb and with indexing inconsistency. Preserve this as a knowledgeable PCH observer's comparison, not a newly verified all-season mechanism history or evidence that a particular part caused the difference. Public event results do not by themselves prove a missing mechanism or diagnose an indexing fault. Further primary CAD, team explanation, appropriately sampled match observations or failure logs would be needed to attribute the gap.

1833 transfer analysis: this is a valuable counterexample question: why can similar high-level functions yield substantially different results? Define comparable operating conditions and measure piece acquisition success by orientation; index completion and retry frequency; handoff dwell; scoring accuracy; traverse/settle time; autonomous success; maintenance downtime; and endgame contribution where valuable. Classify delays by the stage where they occur before changing architecture. A single jam clip is not a season-average failure rate, and a lower overall EPA cannot isolate indexing from alliance, role or endgame effects. The correct next step is a stage-by-stage comparison, not copying the stronger robot's silhouette or asserting inferiority as a fact. This study is deliberately supplemental and partial; it does not claim to add a complete 1261 dossier to the original selection cohort.

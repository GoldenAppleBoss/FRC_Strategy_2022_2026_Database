Concepts:  
●​ Fuel hopper; Similar to 1678 and 254 2017, open top that can also take from rollers on 
the base 
○​ Pros: 
■​ Lots of storage, could store a good bit of fuel, (Likely upwards of 40-50 
fuel, (thanks Adi RI3D concept holding around 50 fuel ))  
■​ Decently simple, like would rank this at a middle/medium difficulty 
■​ Works really well with outpost/human player station intaking due to open 
top, just pour as many as possible in and start shooting when at hub (and 
its active) 
■​ Fastest net cycle time (longer to fill, more to score) 
○​ Cons:  
■​ Takes up a lot of room on robot 
■​ Possible jams (more likely than other options) 
■​ A lot of belts/rollers to function 
■​ If it expands past the frame perimeter its going to get hit 
■​ Could cause issues going under trenches, have to keep it shorter or find a 
way to fold 
●​ Spindexer; 1690 2020 
○​ Pros: 
■​ Less chance of jams (slots for fuel) 
■​ Efficient 
○​ Cons: 
■​ Limited fuel storage max 5 fuel 
■​ Takes up decent bit of space  
■​ Complex 
■​ Requires additional pathing/almost a second mini indexer to get to 
shooter (tower) 
●​ “S- curve” Indexer; 2910 2021 
○​ Pros: 
■​ Takes up smaller space  
■​ Unlikely to jam, fuel follows a rigid path 
■​ Pretty simple 
○​ Cons: 
■​ Less fuel storage, 3 fuel max (maybe 4 but it would be difficult and the 
curve would be have to be pretty harsh (and tall)) 
●​ Multi roller indexer (Vertical and horizontal); 4414 2021

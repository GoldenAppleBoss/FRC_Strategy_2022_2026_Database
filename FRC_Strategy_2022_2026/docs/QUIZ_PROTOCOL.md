# Quiz the evidence, then update the index

Use the user's own questions first. `data/roboreplay_quiz_prompts.jsonl` provides 100 optional prompts tied to the expanded video studies; these are a study aid, not measured model evaluation or an exhaustive question bank.

## Before answering

Identify the season, robot version, match/event stage and the kind of claim being requested. Search the corpus, then open the complete source study and relevant game chapter. A video account remains reported commentary until direct match evidence or a primary team account corroborates it. State when the answer relies on selected passages or a retained synopsis rather than a newly audited entire source.

## What a strong answer includes

- A concrete answer to the question, with a source or timestamp that actually supports it.
- The mechanism or strategic cause: what constraint is being solved and how the proposed explanation follows.
- Conditions and counterexamples that would change the answer.
- The boundary between a team statement, narrator observation, analyst inference and unknown detail.
- Exact year/version and the surrounding alliance role; avoid explaining one mechanism using a different season's geometry.

An answer should not receive credit merely for naming the right team. A correct conclusion with invented team intent, guessed dimensions or an unsupported match narrative is still a gap.

## Turn a weak answer into a research task

Record the original question, the answer given, the user's correction, the affected claim IDs and the source needed to resolve it. Classify the gap as retrieval, missing evidence, version confusion, rule interpretation, numeric/denominator error, visual geometry, or faulty causal reasoning. These require different fixes: adding another paragraph does not solve a wrong version label, and a transcript cannot establish hidden geometry.

Update an existing claim only when the new evidence supports the change. Preserve the older interpretation and its date if it documents how understanding evolved. If a correction is a user observation awaiting corroboration, retain it as such rather than promoting it to an official result. Rebuild the SQL/agent exports and reader after a verified update.

## Known boundaries to probe

The 11 inaccessible RoboReplay videos have no content review. Full-channel enumeration is not certified. The current expanded studies revisit selected passages over 33 inherited full-caption reviews; they are not a new exhaustive annotation of every spoken point or a visual watch. Exact Einstein role timing, some referee interpretations, claimed statistical effects and every robot's intermediate installation history remain open. Ask these questions deliberately, but expect a bounded answer when the necessary evidence is missing.

The 98 focused technical studies fill the earlier missing-study list. They do not transform their source interviews into binders or certify every subsystem and revision. The 27 prior technical-publication review robot-years retain a distinct coverage category.

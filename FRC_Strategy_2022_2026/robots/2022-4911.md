# 2022 · 4911 CyberKnights — QUICKDROP (initially SLAPSHOT)

EPA rank 19, snapshot EPA 51.66. Provisional reference dossier.

Swerve robot with six-bar collector, powered top-roller shooter and extensively revised traversal architecture.

## Drivetrain

SDS MK4i; center of gravity lowered after difficult early driving.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

## Intake

Six-bar intake initially included knockdown rollers for bouncing cargo.

## Transfer

Controlled roller path into launcher; details of all indexer revisions remain unresolved.

## Scoring

Adjustable launcher rails/top roller built from the start; vision-based distance shooting arrived at DCMP.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

## Endgame

Initial flexible climb failed repeatedly; CAD exposes original configuration alongside later design.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

## Controls

Early fender shots preceded integrated Limelight range aiming.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

## Changes and versions

- Glacier Peak: damaged knockdown rollers removed, belt/roller retention strengthened, climber reworked. CAD release explicitly preserves the original climber configuration.

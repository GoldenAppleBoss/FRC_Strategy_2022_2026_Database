# 2022 · 2481 Roboteers — Roboteers 2022

EPA rank 33, snapshot EPA 49.21. Provisional reference dossier.

Five-module octagonal swerve with a truss-driving traversal architecture.

## Drivetrain

Five swerve modules; octagonal frame packages a roughly 44-inch truss-driving climb geometry.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

## Intake

Four-bar pickup changed from Velcro to compliant green wheels; an asymmetric extra wheel prioritizes one of two adjacent balls.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

## Transfer

Rollers lift cargo toward a half-turn turret.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

## Scoring

Heavy main wheel and three compliant back wheels; extra heavy wheels removed to control spin/inertia.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

## Endgame

Deployable front/rear drive wheels travel along the hangar truss; side guides center chassis and sprung carabiner hooks retain traversal.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

## Controls

Manual alignment at the first event; line sensing was proposed, not yet demonstrated in this interview.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

## Changes and versions

- Collector material and flywheel mass revised.
- Separate planned line-sensor alignment from the demonstrated manual sequence.

## Strategic interpretation — analyst inference

This mechanism changes the climb from repeated hook transfers into a constrained driving problem. It also commits the entire frame shape and packaging to that endgame choice.

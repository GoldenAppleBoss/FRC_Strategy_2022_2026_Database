# 2025 · 1690 Orbit — Whisper

EPA rank 4, snapshot EPA 107.72. Provisional reference dossier.

Low-mass differential elevator/arm and universal vacuum gripper optimized for short coral cycles.

## Drivetrain

Custom swerve retained from 2024 with gearing shifted toward acceleration; maximum perimeter and low-mounted motors improve stability.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

## Intake

Collapsing polycarbonate four-bar; floating/slotted upper roller accepts upright coral.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

## Transfer

V-shaped powered conveyor centers coral; four-screw removal and shaft couplers provide service access.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

## Scoring

Differential continuous belt couples elevator and arm while motors stay low. Suction cup serves coral and algae; custom outer silicone seal and twin piston/scotch-yoke vacuum source.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

## Endgame

Simple magnetic tube capture and ratcheted winch; added wider wedges after cage slippage.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

## Controls

Tag cameras maintain sight through final placement; published Einstein account explains station-sharing and match adaptations.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

## Changes and versions

- Cage-retention wedges added during competition.
- Einstein station allocation changed from match 8 onward; this is a tactical evolution, not a new mechanism.

## Strategic interpretation — analyst inference

Vacuum is a mass-distribution choice here. The lightweight high assembly permits aggressive driving, while the vacuum source and differential belt transfer complexity down to the chassis. Reproducing only the suction cup misses the reason the architecture works.

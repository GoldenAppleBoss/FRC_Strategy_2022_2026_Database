## Intake

Collector has large printed mecanum ends; biased flaps and tread combat two-ball jams.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

## Transfer

Tall conveyor and rejection fork; infrared staging pauses a ball long enough for delayed color sensing.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

## Scoring

Two-position pneumatic elevation at fixed speed/distance; brass flywheel inertia and compliant passive Lexan/yoga-mat hood. Powered back roller was unfinished.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

## Endgame

Tilting telescope with passive hooks and rope-induced center-of-gravity movement; lighter plastic outer stage outperformed a heavier replacement that damaged bearings.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

## Controls

Color rejection disabled in autonomous to save time, enabled in teleop. Limited field practice informed simpler shot controls.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

## Changes and versions

- Collector jam countermeasures added.
- Heavier aluminum climb stage was tried and rejected; do not describe it as the final improvement.

## Strategic interpretation — analyst inference

This is a valuable counterexample to copying the most complex elite architecture: development time and practice availability can make a discrete shot map and lower mechanical mass more effective.

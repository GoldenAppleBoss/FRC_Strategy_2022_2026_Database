## 9470: triple-channel scorer to an open drum path

Version: 9470 engineering reference and version history. [Technical source](https://9470techbinder.vercel.app/process/)

**Published construction:** Triple shooter at early events; DCMP drum uses a full-width roller, pivoting hood rollers and three feeder rollers.

**Team-stated reason or evidence boundary:** Higher ceiling without a channel-settling step.

**Engineering analysis — original inference:** The significant change is the removal of restrictive channel interfaces, not simply a larger launch wheel. Dividers can stabilize a piece path while creating an arch or an extra alignment delay; an open drum path can accept a broader front but needs consistent compression across its width. The early triple shooter remains an intentional schedule choice rather than a failed idea in every context. A team with an early event can rationally choose a lower ceiling that preserves practice time, then rebuild once the feed and current demands are understood.

**Proposed validation — not a completed test:** Compare first-shot delay, sustained output and nearly-empty-hopper output across both configurations under the same supply conditions.

**Limits:** The binder is a published configuration snapshot, not an independent durability or match-rate test.

## 9470: reinforce the proven failure while preserving geometry

Version: 9470 engineering reference and version history. [Technical source](https://9470techbinder.vercel.app/process/)

**Published construction:** Worlds intake uses stacked quarter-inch SRP side plates with leaf-spring mounting and reinforced motor plates.

**Team-stated reason or evidence boundary:** DCMP damage made intake survival the next priority.

**Engineering analysis — original inference:** Changing material without changing the mounting boundary may fail to create the intended compliance. A leaf-spring arrangement needs room to bend, a predictable load path and fasteners that do not clamp the compliant region into an unintended rigid joint. Reinforcing the motor plate addresses a concentrated load while preserving the pickup geometry that the drivers already know. This is a useful rebuild principle: once the collection and shot behavior work, the largest improvement may be a structural interface that prevents losing the entire match rather than another nominal-rate optimization.

**Proposed validation — not a completed test:** Measure deflection and residual alignment after repeatable corner hits, and time a side-plate replacement without disturbing the roller assembly.

**Limits:** The binder is a published configuration snapshot, not an independent durability or match-rate test.

## 9470: floor redesign follows the scorer and service needs

Version: 9470 engineering reference and version history. [Technical source](https://9470techbinder.vercel.app/process/)

**Published construction:** Final floor has eight mixed-compliance rollers, one X60 and a two-screw pivot for access.

**Team-stated reason or evidence boundary:** Simpler feeding and fast electronics service.

**Engineering analysis — original inference:** A floor that works for one shooter architecture may create unnecessary volume and transfer stages for another. Mixing hard tubes and compliant contacts can maintain support while encouraging fuel through a problematic region. The service pivot adds a deliberate state outside gameplay: technicians must be able to open the floor, reach electronics and restore alignment quickly. That can improve event reliability more than reducing a small amount of mass. Include cable routing, fastener retention and the post-service check in the architecture record.

**Proposed validation — not a completed test:** Time an actual electrical repair through the pivot, then verify floor alignment and feeding after repeated open/close cycles.

**Limits:** The binder is a published configuration snapshot, not an independent durability or match-rate test.

## 9470: empirical calibration and practice deadline

Version: 9470 engineering reference and version history. [Technical source](https://9470techbinder.vercel.app/process/)

**Published construction:** Interpolated shot map, measured first-shot correction and time-of-flight lookahead; fixed pre-event ship deadline.

**Team-stated reason or evidence boundary:** Preserve practice and tuning time.

**Engineering analysis — original inference:** An empirical map can be a technically rigorous choice when it captures the dominant real-world exit behavior within the available build window. It still needs enough sampling, a stable mechanical reference and a policy for replacing worn surfaces or changing fuel. The deadline is part of the architecture’s feasibility: every additional mechanism competes with the time needed to calibrate and drive it. A borrowed model should include its data-collection procedure and failure fallback rather than promising accuracy from a published algorithm name.

**Proposed validation — not a completed test:** Repeat calibration after surface replacement and at low battery, then validate moving shots outside the original tuning trajectories.

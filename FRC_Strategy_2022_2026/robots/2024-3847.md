# 2024 · 3847 Spectrum   -△◅ — Ultraviolet

EPA rank 22, snapshot EPA 46.92. Provisional reference dossier.

Under-bumper collector, pivoted launcher and reversible backpack amp/trap route.

## Drivetrain

Swerve; centering wedges extend over module areas.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

## Intake

Silicone-wrapped rollers and low-friction wedges center notes.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

## Transfer

Two-roller feeder can reverse into the backpack path.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

## Scoring

Sector-gear pivot with steel reinforcement; asymmetrical wheel arrangement adds spin.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

## Endgame

Tilted hook elevator lifts robot; backpack raises and reverses note into trap.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

## Controls

Four Limelights localize; absolute pivot sensing controls shot angle.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

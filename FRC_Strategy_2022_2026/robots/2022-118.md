# 2022 · 118 Robonauts — Horizon

EPA rank 22, snapshot EPA 50.9. Provisional reference dossier.

Turreted swerve scorer with compliant collector and rotating climber.

## Drivetrain

SDS MK4; L3 gearing changed to L1 after Texas State for acceleration under load.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

## Intake

Foam-tile dead-axle roller, sacrificial polycarbonate shield and tubing compliance; two pneumatic positions protect the deployment cylinder.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

## Transfer

Four omni rollers feed a kicker that isolates cargo from the spinning flywheel.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

## Scoring

Two-Falcon main wheel, powered upper roller, turret and rack-adjusted hood. Coarser rack addressed tooth skipping.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

## Endgame

Three-motor rotating mechanism with deploy motor, locking clips and center-of-gravity shift; interview reports roughly 5–6-second climbs.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

## Controls

Turret/range control decouples collection heading from scoring aim.

Coverage: documented synopsis.

## Changes and versions

- Post-Texas-State drive ratio reduction.
- Hood rack revised; rotating climb swing transfer improved. IRI interview is postseason.

## Strategic interpretation — analyst inference

Collector compliance and acceleration are throughput mechanisms. Turret sophistication is valuable only when the intake, isolation kicker and chassis consistently supply clean shots.
